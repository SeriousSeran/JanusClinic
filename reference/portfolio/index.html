01 · Design character

Medical clarity.
Aesthetic sensitivity.

The visual language balances a professional medical practice with the warmth of personal care. Preserve that balance before adding a new visual idea.

Clinical confidence

Let evidence lead.

Practitioner credentials, clear treatment explanations, honest expectations, and readable content carry the authority.

Quiet warmth

Keep the canvas calm.

Ivory, warm stone, charcoal, and restrained gold. Generous space and light serif headings give the photography room to breathe.

Consistent structure

Repeat the familiar.

Shared gutters, measured type, small uppercase labels, fine borders, and direct calls to action connect every page.

04 · Reusable patterns

Build from the existing grammar.

The components below demonstrate the rules behind the pages. Use a nearby existing page as your first reference.

Actions & navigation

Primary actions use charcoal and ivory. Secondary actions use a fine border. Standard button padding is 14px × 24px, type is 13px / 500, and corners are 2px.

Space & proportion

  • Content container: 1200px maximum, including gutters.
  • Gutters: 40px desktop, 24px mobile.
  • Standard section rhythm: 96px desktop, 64px mobile.
  • Spacing base: 4px; grids generally use 24–64px gaps.
  • Editorial article copy is limited to 72ch.

Photography & cards

Use original clinic, practitioner, treatment, and product photography. Preserve image proportions and focal points. Cards use imagery, fine separation, and whitespace instead of heavy shadows or rounded panels.

07 · Contributor handoff

Before changing a page.

The repository’s AGENTS.md and docs/design-identity.md turn this visual reference into practical instructions for future agents and developers.

A repeatable review

  1. Find the closest page template in this portfolio and inspect it on desktop and mobile.
  2. Reuse the palette, font pairing, spacing, proportions, and button hierarchy.
  3. Keep the medical voice clear and factual. Use verified credentials, prices, and treatment information.
  4. Check keyboard focus, labels, contrast, image cropping, links, and small-screen overflow.
  5. Run the archive, server, and browser checks. Compare screenshots before accepting a visual change.

Preserve the identity

Keep the original logo, real photography, warm surfaces, normal-weight serif headings, and measured whitespace. Avoid unrelated gradients, neon accents, oversized pills, heavy shadows, or an invented logo.

The original pages are a rendered capture with embedded Next.js data, not maintained component source. New reference files live outside that capture so refreshing it does not erase this guide. Rebuild a page as editable source when a substantial change needs it.
