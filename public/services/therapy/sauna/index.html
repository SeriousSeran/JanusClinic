Overview

Janus Therapy offers both infrared (IR) sauna and traditional steam sauna as standalone wellness treatments and as complementary therapy alongside body contouring programmes. Infrared saunas penetrate deeper into tissue than conventional heat, promoting circulation, muscle recovery, and a genuine detoxification response. Steam sauna provides traditional deep cleansing and relaxation benefits.

Targets

Muscle tension, stress, detoxification, post-workout recovery

How it works

01

Preparation

Hydrate well before your session. Remove jewellery. A towel and change of clothes are provided.

02

Session

IR or steam session typically lasts 20–40 minutes at the recommended temperature.

03

Cool Down

A cool shower post-session is recommended. Rehydrate with water.

Ideal candidates

  • Anyone seeking relaxation and stress relief
  • Those with muscle tension or post-exercise recovery needs
  • Clients on a body contouring programme (supports lymphatic drainage)

This is not suitable if…

  • Cardiovascular conditions, consult your doctor before sauna use
  • Pregnancy
  • Active fever or illness

Results & timeline

Immediate relaxation and sense of wellbeing. Cumulative benefits with regular use.

Recommended sessions

As needed. Weekly use offers cumulative wellness benefits.
