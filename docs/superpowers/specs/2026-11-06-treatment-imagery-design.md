# Treatment imagery — mixed photography and illustration

## Intent and approved approach

Add a relevant image to every treatment in Wellness, Salon and Cosmetic Care, helping visitors understand the offering while preserving the approved Janus ivory/sage/teal identity, serif typography and restrained glass controls. The owner selected a mix of existing photographs and AI illustrations.

## Approaches considered

- Genuine photography only: accurate source context, but the available library does not cover every treatment.
- Mixed photography and clearly labelled AI illustration (selected): reuse suitable existing images and fill the gaps with a coordinated editorial series featuring Sri Lankan adults. Real clinic and equipment photographs retain their actual context.

## Proposed small change

- Wellness and Cosmetic Care treatment cards receive consistently cropped images above their existing content. Expanded information retains the image context; booking selection may reuse its treatment thumbnail without changing the booking flow.
- Every Salon service receives a compact thumbnail within the existing searchable catalog. Length/size variants and closely related services share an appropriate service-family image; unique images for every price variant are unnecessary.
- Use existing treatment photographs only where they accurately correspond to the named service. Missing massage, beauty and assessment illustrations use natural skin texture, modest styling and soft rose/ivory backgrounds compatible with the current palette. No before/after, outcomes, patient identities or invented facilities/equipment.
- Show a visible “AI-generated illustration” caption for generated treatment images, with descriptive alt text. Real photos must not be claimed to show a specific therapist, patient, device or room unless verified. Any illustrative facility-related imagery is described as conceptual, never as an actual clinic facility.
- Keep a single editable image/provenance mapping, local optimized WebP assets, responsive crops and lazy loading. Shared images avoid redundant downloads. Missing assets get a readable fallback; prices and booking controls stay usable.

## Scope and checks

Keep prices, provider distinctions, consultation policy and the memory-only booking demo unchanged. Preserve the original capture and historical portfolio. Check coverage for every visible treatment, image loading and correct prefixes on local and GitHub Pages previews, generated-image disclosure, mobile layout, and existing booking behavior. Publish the updated preview and rebuild the source handoff ZIP after verification.

This note proposes the image placement and service-family reuse; the owner's selection of the mixed approach is already recorded above. No product implementation has been made in this step.
