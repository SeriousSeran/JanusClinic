# Janus product context

Janus Estheti Clinique serves customers exploring Wellness, Salon and Cosmetic Care in Sri Lanka. The current application is a public design-review preview, with LKR pricing and a memory-only fictional customer/booking journey. Browsing is free; identification belongs to booking. The welcome/account area stays above the three care destinations.

Authoritative requirements and owner decisions: [website direction](docs/website-direction.md), [Wellness brief review](docs/wellness-brief-review.md), [approved usability review](docs/reviews/2026-11-06-website-improvements.md), and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). This entry document links those sources rather than duplicating their commercial policies.

Structured sources: `frontend/data/wellness.json`, `content/salon/catalog.draft.json`, and `frontend/data/clinic.json`. Treat source/confirmation notes as part of the content. Never invent hours approval, clinical techniques, duration, preparation, results or customer reviews.

Real authentication, OTP, database records, clinic notifications/confirmation, staff access, payments and subscription credits belong to the separate Lovable/live-backend phase. No change to a demo label activates those capabilities.
